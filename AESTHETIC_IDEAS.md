# 30 ideas to make Sunnyvale more beautiful (passthrough / Stark mode)

Written 22 Oct 2026 for Bastian to choose from. None of these are built, except that the prepared Bubble look already has a very gentle float (close to #8). Effort: S = an evening, M = a day, L = several days. Cost = how much it could slow the headset.

## Top 5 picks

1. **Soft light that follows the room (#1).** Windows pick up a warm or cool tint from the real room's light, so they feel like they belong in your space. (M, low)
2. **Windows that breathe in and out (#6).** New windows grow from a small glowing bead, and closing pops them like a soap bubble with a faint shimmer. (S, low)
3. **Focus mode dims the world (#16).** When the focus timer runs, passthrough darkens slightly with a soft vignette, and only the window you're working in stays bright. (S, low)
4. **Paper-thin depth (#3).** Each window gets a very subtle second layer behind the text, so it reads like a thick pane of glass with real depth as you move your head. (M, low)
5. **Sound design (#21).** Soft glass "tinks" when you touch buttons, a low whoosh when you move the group, and a quiet chime when Claude starts speaking. (S, low)

## Light and materials

1. **Room-matched tint:** sample the passthrough brightness and warmth now and then, and tint window glass to match. (M, low)
2. **Rim light that follows your hand:** the window edge nearest your hand glows a little brighter, like light catching glass. (S, low)
3. **Glass depth layer:** a faint offset copy of the window's edge behind it gives a thick-glass parallax. (M, low)
4. **Frosted blur behind windows:** blur whatever is behind a window, the way frosted glass does. Passthrough can't be blurred in WebXR, but the virtual objects behind can. (L, high)
5. **Colour themes:** Coral (Claude), Ocean, Forest, Monochrome and Film-noir, chosen in WORLD › LOOK. (S, low)

## Motion

6. **Bubble open and close:** a window grows from a bead, and closing pops it like a soap bubble with a shimmer of particles. (S, low)
7. **Magnetic snapping:** windows gently snap into neat rows or arcs when you let go near another window. (M, low)
8. **Lazy float:** an idle window drifts a few millimetres and turns slightly toward you over time. (S, low)
9. **Parallax title:** a window's title floats a tiny bit in front of its body text, so it shifts as you move your head. (S, low)
10. **Smooth follow:** pinned windows ease after you when you walk away and settle when you stop, like a pet. (M, low)

## The room around you

11. **Ambient dust in light beams:** a few slow, glowing motes drift through the air near your windows, barely visible. (S, med)
12. **A soft floor glow:** a faint pool of light on the real floor under your desk area marks your study zone. (S, low)
13. **Constellation of stashed windows:** stashed windows hang as tiny glowing stars high up, and you pull one down to open it. (M, low)
14. **Window shadows on the real floor:** a soft, blurry shadow under each window grounds it in the room. (M, med)
15. **Light ribbons between linked things:** a thin glowing thread connects a quote to its source, or a mind map to its thesis paragraph. (M, low)

## Focus and moods

16. **Focus vignette:** passthrough dims slightly while focus time runs, and the active window stays bright. (S, low)
17. **Break mode bloom:** during a break, the glass turns warmer and softer, and the menu fills with colour. (S, low)
18. **Time-of-day palette:** cooler, crisper glass in the morning, warmer and dimmer glass at night. (S, low)
19. **Deep work aura:** after 25 minutes of focus, a calm ring slowly grows around the window you're working in, a quiet reward. (S, low)
20. **Night mode:** a red-shifted, low-brightness look for late-night reading. (S, low)

## Sound

21. **Glass interface sounds:** soft tinks, whooshes and chimes instead of the current beeps. (S, low)
22. **Spatial sound:** each window's sounds come from where the window is. (S, low)
23. **Ambience player:** optional rain, café or library sounds in Stark mode, at a low volume. (S, low)

## Claude's presence

24. **Avatar trail:** the spark leaves a faint coral trail when it flies to point at something. (S, low)
25. **Claude's writing light:** when Claude edits a window, a soft light sweeps across the changed lines. (S, low)
26. **Speaking ripples:** while Claude talks, gentle rings ripple across the nearest window's glass. (M, med)

## Text and readability

27. **Beautiful typography:** a refined serif for reading and thesis windows, a clean sans for interface, with proper line height and margins. (S, low)
28. **Highlight glow:** highlighted quotes glow softly in the text like a marker under light. (S, low)
29. **Gaze focus:** the window you look at gets very slightly brighter and the others relax, guiding your eye. (M, low)
30. **Curved reading panels:** long documents bend gently around you like a cinema screen, so the edges stay sharp and easy to read. (L, med)
