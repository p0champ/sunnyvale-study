Most textures, the night sky and these models are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0): barrel stove, plastic crate, wooden picnic table, small wooden table, wine bottles, plastic monobloc chair, 8mm filmstrip projector, covered car, chain-link fence.
